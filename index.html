How it works

  1. DropOne image or a whole batch. They are processed one after another.
  2. WaitThe image is scanned on your own device. Flat logos are cut by color in a blink, photos go through an AI model.
  3. Compare and saveDrag the slider, pick a new background if you want one, download the PNG.
Is it really free and private?

Yes. There is no server: the image never leaves your device, there is no account, no watermark, and no limit on resolution beyond your device memory. The only download is the AI model itself, fetched once and kept in your browser.

Which models does it use?

Photos use ISNet (DIS, Apache-2.0, MIT-licensed ONNX conversion), a model built for cluttered scenes and fine edges. It runs on your graphics card through WebGPU when available, otherwise on the CPU. Person mode uses MODNet and Quick mode uses U2-Netp (both Apache-2.0). Logos and screenshots with a plain or smoothly shaded background skip the AI and are cut by color, which gives pixel-clean edges.

Why is the first photo slower?

ISNet is an 88 MB download (176 MB when it runs on WebGPU) from huggingface.co, only the first time. After that it comes from your browser cache. Without WebGPU, expect a while per photo. Quick mode works immediately and offline.

Does it work offline?

After the first visit the page, the runtime and the two small models are cached, so Quick and Person mode and flat-color cuts work without internet. The BiRefNet model works offline once it has been downloaded.
