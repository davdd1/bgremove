How it works

  1. DropOne image or a whole batch. They are processed one after another.
  2. WaitA segmentation model runs on your own device, a few seconds per image.
  3. Compare and saveDrag the slider, pick a new background if you want one, download the PNG.
Is it really free and private?

Yes. There is no server: the model is downloaded once and runs locally with WebAssembly. No image is sent anywhere, there is no account, no watermark, and no limit on resolution beyond your device memory.

Which models does it use?

U2-Netp for general subjects and MODNet for portraits, both Apache-2.0 licensed, so results can be used commercially. It does well on clear subjects and worse on hair, glass and cluttered scenes.

Does it work offline?

After the first visit the page, the runtime and the models are cached by your browser, so it keeps working without internet.

No tracking. Models: U2-Net and MODNet (Apache-2.0). Runtime: ONNX Runtime Web (MIT). Motion by Motion.dev (MIT).
