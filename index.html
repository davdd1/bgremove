How to remove the background from an image for free

  1. Drop one or many images onto the box above.
  2. Wait a few seconds per image. An AI segmentation model runs on your own device.
  3. Drag the slider to compare before and after, optionally pick a new background, then download the transparent PNG.

Is it really free and private?

Yes. There is no server: the model is downloaded once and runs locally with WebAssembly. No image is sent anywhere, there is no account, no watermark and no limit on resolution beyond your device memory.

Which models does it use?

U2-Netp for general subjects and MODNet for portraits, both Apache-2.0 licensed, so results can be used commercially. Segmentation quality is good for clear subjects and weaker on hair, glass and cluttered scenes.

Does it work offline?

After the first visit the page, the runtime and the models are cached by your browser, so it keeps working without internet.

Open tools, no tracking. Models: U2-Net (Apache-2.0), MODNet (Apache-2.0). Runtime: ONNX Runtime Web (MIT).
